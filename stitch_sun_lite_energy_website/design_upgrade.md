## Full Product Requirements Document (PRD) + Design DNA + UX/Motion Specification

**Document status:** Design upgrade specification  
**Source basis:** Existing SUN LITE LTD HTML website and supplied petroleum/industrial reference image  
**Primary objective:** Transform the current petroleum-products supply website into a premium, globally credible, Apple-standard digital experience without inventing unsupported company facts.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

SUN LITE LTD is presented in the current website as a **petroleum products supply company based in Lagos, Nigeria**, with **Diesel as its principal product**, alongside Fuel and Kerosene supply and marine diesel delivery.

The website should evolve from a conventional information website into a **global-standard Oil & Gas / Energy supply corporate platform** that communicates:

- Reliability
- Operational confidence
- Professionalism
- Supply capability
- Marine readiness
- Customer trust
- Safety and responsible operations
- Nigerian roots with a globally credible presentation

The positioning should be **global-ready**, not falsely claim that SUN LITE LTD already operates globally unless the company confirms international operations, offices, certifications, fleet size, storage capacity, trading volumes, or geographic coverage.

### Core brand proposition

> **Delivering Energy. Building Trust.**

### Recommended supporting proposition

> **Reliable petroleum products supply for businesses, institutions, fuel stations and marine operations.**

# 30. Motion Design System

## Philosophy

> Motion should explain hierarchy, not entertain the user.

## Global transitions

Default:

`250–400ms`

Easing:

```css
cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
```

## Page entrance

Initial load:

1. Header
2. Hero eyebrow
3. Hero headline
4. Supporting copy
5. CTA

## Scroll reveal

Use IntersectionObserver.

Elements enter from:

- opacity: 0 → 1
- translateY: 24px → 0

Duration:

`600–800ms`

## Cards

On hover:

- translateY(-4px)
- image scale(1.03)
- subtle shadow increase

## Buttons

On hover:

- arrow moves 3–5px
- background transition
- slight brightness shift

Avoid:

- spinning logos
- bouncing buttons
- excessive text animations
- continuous floating effects

# 45. Apple-Standard Design Principles

## 01 — Less, but better

Every element must earn its place.

## 02 — Strong hierarchy

The user should immediately understand:

**Who → What → Why → Action**

## 03 — Whitespace

Whitespace communicates confidence.

## 04 — Progressive disclosure

Do not expose every detail at once.

## 05 — Direct manipulation

Buttons and interactive elements should provide immediate feedback.

## 06 — Motion with purpose

Motion should explain state changes.

## 07 — Visual consistency

One icon family, one button system, one spacing system.
