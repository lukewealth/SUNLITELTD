## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the institutional authority, immense infrastructure, and operational precision of downstream and marine energy distribution. The identity marries heavy industrial logistics with the restrained discipline of Swiss modernism and high-end editorial finance journals.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Industrial Precision**: Monolithic structural layouts, architectural rule lines, sharp grid alignments, and high-legibility telemetry that project operational scale without visual clutter.
- **Editorial Subtlety**: Clean negative space balanced with high-contrast type scales, creating an authoritative executive presence for institutional clients, maritime operators, and corporate procurement directors.
- **African Energy Modernity**: Rich deep hydrocarbon charcoals punctuated by calculated strikes of refined refined-petroleum amber—communicating heat, kinetic energy, and liquid assets.
- **Absolute Realism**: Pure SVG geometric iconography, data-dense metric units, coordinate readouts, and austere technical layouts. Strictly zero whimsical consumer elements or emojis.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the user in the physical reality of refined petroleum, marine freight hulls, and boardroom clarity.

- **Primary (`#0B1015`)**: Deep Bitumen Dark. Used for high-level headers, primary surfaces in inverted sections, dominant call-to-action buttons, and high-impact framing borders.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Refined Amber / Energy Gold. Applied sparingly to critical metrics, status indicators, live voyage tracking, active pipeline/bunkering states, and primary navigational focus states. Never used as large background washes.
- **Tertiary (`#1E293B`)**: Marine Slate. Powers table headers, secondary typography, card borders, and auxiliary interactive modules.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Technical Slate. Reserved for supporting specs, unit labels (e.g., MT, BBL, DWT), subheaders, and architectural divider rules.
- **Canvas & Surface Base**:
  - Primary Canvas: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Paper)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F8FAFC` (Off-white Tanker Gray)
  - Dark Surface Container: `#111823` (Petroleum Sub-surface for dark mode/terminal panels)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a dialogue between corporate poise, editorial scale, and logistical instrumentation.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Rendered in aggressive, architectural weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter-tracking to mimic modern industrial engineering marks. Display type balances executive weight with pristine clarity.
- **Body Text (Inter)**: Deployed across all descriptive paragraphs and legal clauses for maximum legibility in complex operational contexts.
- **Labels, Telemetry, & Operational Metrics (Space Grotesk)**: Used for metadata, shipping codes, tanker tonnages, coordinate telemetry, product specifications, and status chips. Set in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`) when acting as section headers or data tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a hybrid editorial grid prioritizing structural alignment and asymmetric tension.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1440px max-width container, featuring asymmetrical layout distributions (e.g., 5-column technical dossier summaries adjacent to 7-column operational telemetry charts).
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid scaling with 24px margins, reflowing side-by-side modules into unified operational panels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: 20px edge margins. Stacked columnar progression with clear hair-line divider separations.

### Cadence and Rhythm
Spacing follows an architectural 8pt baseline scale. Section gaps rely on expansive vertical spacing (`space-xl` scaled dynamically) to establish institutional gravitas, while internal card padding remains compact and data-dense (`space-md` to `space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids consumer drop-shadows and blurred glass surfaces, relying instead on structural boundaries, tonal stepping, and hairline rules.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base`: `#FFFFFF` for primary editorial reading areas.
  - `Card Surface`: `#F8FAFC` cleanly bounded by razor-thin borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
  - `Inverted / Deep Marine Container`: `#0B1015` with internal borders of `1px solid #1E293B`, reserved for fuel discharge specs, live bunkering feeds, and corporate statements.
- **Hairline Outlines**: Boundaries define spatial distribution. Thin `1px` lines in `#E2E8F0` (light context) or `#1E293B` (dark context) anchor every card, header strip, and data module.
- **Operational Shadows**: Reserved strictly for floating elements like navigation bars and modal overlays. Values are ultra-diffuse and desaturated: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 16, 21, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The interface projects solidity and engineering rigor through low roundedness.

- **Level 1 (Soft)**: Base elements feature a clean `0.25rem` (4px) border radius. This subtle curvature relieves mechanical harshness while preserving the feeling of solid, engineered components.
- **Containers & Data Cards**: Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Data Badges & Micro-tags**: `0.25rem` (4px). Avoid fully rounded pill badges; maintain structured rectangles for telemetry, grade codes (e.g., AGO, PMS, DPK, MGO), and vessel classifications.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational**: Solid Bitumen (`#0B1015`) background with crisp white typography, `0.25rem` radius, `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal padding. Accompanied by sharp 16px geometric arrow icons. Hover state shifts to `#1E293B` with 0.15s ease.
- **Secondary Energy**: Outlined in `1.5px solid #0B1015` with black text, transitioning on hover to `#0B1015` fill and white text.
- **Tertiary Amber Accent**: Used exclusively for critical transaction points (e.g., RFQ submissions, Terminal Slot Booking). Background `#D97706`, white text. Hover state `#B45309`.

### Status & Spec Chips
- Compact, monospaced layout using `Space Grotesk`. Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0B1015`.
- **Operational Status Indicators**: Feature a 6px geometric square dot (not a rounded circle): `#D97706` for En Route/Pumping, `#10B981` for Discharged/Available, `#64748B` for Dry Dock/Reserved.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `4px` radius. Typographic placeholder set in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border sharpens to `#0B1015` with a zero-blur outline (`2px solid rgba(11, 16, 21, 0.12)`).
- Input labels leverage `label-caps` in `#475569` positioned strictly outside the input container.

### Logistics & Vessel Cards
- Asymmetrical top-bordered panels: Features a 2px top accent line in `#0B1015` or `#D97706`.
- Internal divisions use dashed hairline rules (`1px dashed #E2E8F0`) to cleanly separate cargo manifests, destination ports (e.g., Lagos, Port Harcourt, Warri), and metric volume specs.

### Data Tables (Marine & Commercial Specs)
- High-density striping with `#F8FAFC` on alternating rows. 
- Headers styled with `label-caps`, `#475569`, tracking `0.12em`, and bottom-bordered with `2px solid #0B1015`.
- Numerical metrics align right using tabular figures for precise auditing.

### Iconography Rules
- Strict use of 1.5px to 2px stroke-width geometric line vectors (Heroicons style).
- Rendered in either `#0B1015`, `#64748B`, or `#D97706`. No solid filled colorful icons and strictly zero emojis.
