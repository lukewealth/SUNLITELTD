Who We Are

Dependable petroleum supply, built around your needs.

SUN LITE LTD is a petroleum products supply company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We focus primarily on Diesel supply while also supplying Fuel and Kerosene.

✓Diesel first: dependable diesel supply for homes, businesses, organisations and other approved customer locations.
✓Fuel: delivery is focused on fuel stations.
✓Kerosene: available for fuel stations and personal individuals with big tanks suitable for storage.
✓Marine: diesel supply to all kinds of ships and vessels.
